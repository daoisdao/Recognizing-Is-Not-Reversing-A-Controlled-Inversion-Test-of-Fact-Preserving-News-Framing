## News sources

Original news contributions retain their upstream licenses.
[Wikinews copyright policy](https://en.wikinews.org/wiki/Wikinews:Copyright)
specifies:

- Material published after December 16, 2024: CC BY 4.0.
- Material published after September 25, 2005 and before December 16, 2024:
  [CC BY 2.5](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.5/).
- Material published before September 25, 2005: public domain.

Attribute source text to **Wikinews** using the titles and revision URLs in
[sources_attribution.json](sources_attribution.json). Dataset processing includes
markup removal, canonicalization, fact extraction, framing transformations, and
model reconstruction. Retain source attribution and this modification notice
when redistributing derivatives.

Source records retain their historical “CC BY 2.5” labels; the applicable
upstream terms depend on the publication and contribution history.
The dataset contains article text rather than Wikinews images or logos.
